Activity 3 — Error Detective

Study12 min · pairs

Five types of AI error:

  • Hallucination: invented facts, names, quotes or sources (fabricated).
  • Outdated: true before, but not now.
  • Unsupported: no evidence; watch for “always”, “all”, “studies show”.
  • Bias: a stereotype, or only one side of the story.
  • Calculation: a wrong number, date or step.

Read each AI output and the check result. Choose the error type. Goal: 8 of 10.
